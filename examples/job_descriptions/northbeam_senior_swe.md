# Senior Software Engineer — Payments Platform

**Company:** Northbeam Payments  
**Location:** Remote (EU)  
**Type:** Full-time

## About the role

We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to own critical services in our payments platform. You will design and operate distributed systems that move money reliably at scale.

## Responsibilities

- Own end-to-end design and delivery of payment orchestration services
- Drive reliability improvements across multi-region deployments
- Partner with product and risk teams on cross-functional initiatives
- Mentor mid-level engineers and raise engineering bar through design reviews
- Improve observability, SLOs, and incident response for high-throughput APIs

## Requirements

- 5+ years building backend services in Python or Go
- Hands-on experience with distributed systems and eventual consistency
- Experience with streaming platforms (Kafka or equivalent)
- Strong production ownership: on-call, incident leadership, postmortems
- Comfortable with ambiguity in a fast-paced environment

## Nice to have

- Payments or fintech domain experience
- Prior work on multi-region active-passive or active-active architectures
- Platform/developer experience investments

## Benefits

- Competitive salary + equity
- Remote-first culture
- Learning stipend
