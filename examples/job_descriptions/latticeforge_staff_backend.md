# Staff Backend Engineer — Developer Platform

**Company:** Latticeforge  
**Location:** Hybrid — Berlin  
**Type:** Full-time

## Role summary

Latticeforge is looking for a Staff Backend Engineer to lead our internal developer platform. You will set technical direction for CI/CD, service templates, and onboarding tooling used by 200+ engineers.

## What you'll do

- Define multi-year platform strategy and roadmap
- Own golden-path service scaffolding and paved-road tooling
- Drive organizational change through RFCs and working groups
- Coach senior engineers; influence without direct line management
- Measure developer productivity and reduce time-to-first-PR

## Must-have qualifications

- 8+ years software engineering; 2+ years at staff/principal scope
- Deep experience building internal platforms / developer tooling
- Excellent written communication for RFCs and design docs
- Track record of mentoring and raising org-wide engineering standards
- Experience with Kubernetes and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP)

## Nice-to-haves

- Prior IC→Staff transition story in a growth-stage company
- Contributions to open-source developer tools
- Background in stream processing is a plus but not required

## Benefits

- Generous parental leave
